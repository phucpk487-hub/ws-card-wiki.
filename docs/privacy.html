Privacy Policy

Weiss Tool Box is an unofficial, fan-made tool for browsing and organising your own Weiss Schwarz card sets and decks. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bushiroad or any rights holder.

Data the app collects

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no server. We do not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

Card sets you import (from your own files) and the decks you build are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded. Backup files you create are saved to your device's Downloads or Documents folder, or shared through the share sheet you choose. Uninstalling the app removes the stored data.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or accounts.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
